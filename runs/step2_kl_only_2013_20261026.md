# Step 2: matched five-epoch KL comparison

The controlled pilot completed successfully. Adding the existing 0.01 KL
early-stopping threshold changed neither learning nor evaluated returns in this
experiment: the threshold never triggered, no optimizer steps were skipped, and
the final policy and learning-state fingerprints matched in all three seed pairs.

This is a nonbinding control result. It is not evidence that KL guarding improves
performance, nor evidence that it cannot matter later in training or on other years.
The normal training defaults remain unchanged.

## Protocol

| Setting | Baseline | Guarded |
|---|---|---|
| Maximum actor epochs per update | 5 | 5 |
| KL threshold | Disabled | 0.01 |
| Initial training seeds | 1, 2, 3 | Same matched seeds |
| Training year | 2013 | 2013 |
| Annual trajectories per update | 6 | 6 |
| Updates per arm and seed | 3 | 3 |
| Annual training episodes per arm and seed | 18 | 18 |
| Action bins | 50 | 50 |
| Sampled evaluation repeats | 3 | 3 |

Both arms retain hidden layers 128/128, actor and critic learning rates 0.0003,
five critic epochs, clipping 0.2, entropy coefficient 0.001, gamma 0.99, GAE lambda
0.95, ValueNorm, unit service penalty weights, the same seasonal constraints,
storage-dependent head, and annual initial-storage closure. The same future-inflow
feasibility layer remains in use. The repository's YAML action-bin default remains
200; this pilot retains the preceding experiment's 50-bin grid.

The only optimization setting changed between arms is `target_kl`. Diagnostics
are enabled in both. Each arm performs fresh physical simulations; evaluation
results are not copied between arms. Sampled evaluations use the same action-seed
grid across arms and checkpoints. Modes are evaluated at episodes 0, 6, 12 and 18;
three sampled evaluations are performed at episodes 0 and 18.

Comparisons use the final current policies at the same episode budget, rather
than the best in-sample checkpoint selected separately for each arm. This pilot
does not evaluate validation or test years. All seeds are initialized independently,
and each guarded arm must match its baseline's initial learning-state fingerprint.
The three seed jobs were executed in parallel, with their two arms run sequentially.

## Results

Both arms produced the following identical final discounted net returns:

| Training seed | Baseline sampled mean | Guarded sampled mean | Baseline deterministic | Guarded deterministic | Steps skipped by guard |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 1 | -37.570885 | -37.570885 | 0.194180 | 0.194180 | 0 |
| 2 | -35.804736 | -35.804736 | 9.155297 | 9.155297 | 0 |
| 3 | -38.627687 | -38.627687 | 11.721734 | 11.721734 | 0 |

Every paired sampled-repeat difference is exactly zero. The mean guarded-minus-
baseline difference, its sample SD and its standard error are all zero within
each seed and across the three paired seed estimates. These zeros arise from
identical policies and matched evaluation randomness. They do not establish a
general zero effect across longer training schedules or different scenarios.

| Check | Seed 1 | Seed 2 | Seed 3 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Largest reported update-end actor mean KL, either arm | 0.00489849 | 0.00536977 | 0.00487132 |
| Guarded threshold crossings | 0 | 0 | 0 |
| Epochs omitted by guard | 0 | 0 | 0 |
| Actor optimizer steps, each actor in either arm | 15 | 15 | 15 |
| Initial learning-state fingerprints match | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Final actor fingerprints match | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Final learning-state fingerprints match | Yes | Yes | Yes |

The guard checks KL after every actor epoch, not just at the reported update
endpoint. All 225 guarded actor epochs were executed without a threshold crossing.
Both arms therefore took 225 actor optimizer steps across the three seeds. The
complete experiment executed 108 annual training episodes; all completed without
a recorded hard-constraint failure.

Fingerprint checks cover actor and critic weights, Adam states, ValueNorm,
network modes, Python/NumPy/Torch/year RNG states, and iteration/update/step
counters. Histories and elapsed time are intentionally excluded. The final
fingerprint match is stronger evidence of an unchanged learning trajectory than
similar reward curves alone.

A crossing on the fifth epoch would also omit zero later epochs. The driver
therefore reports threshold crossings separately from actual omitted epochs.
In this pilot neither occurred.

## Interpretation

At this five-epoch budget, the proposed 0.01 guard did not become an effective
intervention during the first three updates. There is consequently no measured
benefit or harm to attribute to the guard here. The substantial gap between sampled
and deterministic returns persists in both arms; adding this inactive guard does
not address that behavior.

The study is an early-training screen on one training year and three seeds, not
a long-run or held-out performance study. The inspected older multi-year logs
had rare threshold exceedances much later in training, so this short pilot does
not rule out a later role for the guard. A tighter threshold would be a separate
experiment; it should not be presented as a validated remedy based on this result.

The original five-versus-twenty-epoch budget comparison remains available as a
different experiment. Its results must not be interpreted as isolating KL stopping:
that experiment changes the optimization budget as well as the KL setting.

## Implementation and verification

`examples/compare_cascade_policy_budget.py --kl-only` selects the matched profile.
It rejects an incompatible epoch count, persists arm-tagged step-1 diagnostics,
records full common configuration and source/data hashes, checks initial state
identity, and emits per-seed paired comparisons plus an across-seed summary.
It compares final current policies and records omitted epochs separately from
threshold flags. The older default budget comparison still uses five versus
twenty epochs when `--kl-only` is omitted.

32 focused tests passed: 11 new comparison tests plus 21 existing policy-budget
and step-1 diagnostic tests. Coverage includes matched budgets, state fingerprints,
paired uncertainty, malformed or mismatched evaluation grids, last-epoch crossings,
diagnostic persistence, the legacy comparison, and output overwrite protection.
`git diff --check` passed. Source code hashes in all three real runs were verified
against the working tree, and all three runs used matching code/data hashes and
physical configuration. No core optimizer, reward, physics, or YAML default was
changed in this step.

Raw per-arm elapsed times are recorded. The seed jobs ran concurrently, so these
times are not a controlled benchmark of KL-check overhead.
